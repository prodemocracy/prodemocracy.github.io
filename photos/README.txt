Politician photos

Put a photo here named with the politician's FEC candidate ID, and it replaces the
"Photo coming soon" placeholder on their page and on the compare page.

  1. Open the politician on the site. The ID is the last part of the address:
     .../#/p/H2NY10092  ->  the file is H2NY10092.jpg
  2. Save it as .jpg (lowercase). A portrait shape (4 wide by 5 tall) looks best; it's
     cropped to fit. Keep files small (under 200 KB; about 400 x 500 pixels is plenty).
  3. Upload it into this photos folder on GitHub (Add file > Upload files).

Use photos you have the right to publish. Official House and Senate portraits are
generally U.S. government works and free to use. News and agency photos usually
aren't, even when they're easy to find online.
